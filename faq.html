Frequently asked questions

Do my images get uploaded to a server?

No. Inkmark runs in your browser, and your image stays on your device. There is no server that receives it.

Is Inkmark free to use?

Yes. There is no account to create and nothing to pay for.

Which image formats are supported?

Any image your browser can open, including PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF. Annotated images are exported as PNG.

How do I save my marked-up image?

Click Download (or press Ctrl+S) to save a PNG file. To paste it somewhere instead, click Copy (or press Ctrl+C with nothing selected).
